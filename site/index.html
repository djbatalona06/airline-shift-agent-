Shift Agent

Watches your work portal for open shifts, checks each one against your availability, and asks before it claims anything.

The problem

Good shifts get posted at 3am and are gone by morning. Refreshing a portal all day isn't a plan, and neither is losing sleep over it.

What it does

Watches

Polls on a jittered interval, only during hours you choose, with quiet hours respected.

Filters

Checks availability, existing trips, minimum rest, weekly caps, home base and position class.

Asks first

Sends the shift to Telegram with Confirm and Skip. It only claims on an explicit yes.

Knows when to stop

Three failed attempts on a shift and it gives up rather than hammering the portal.

What it refuses to do

Solve captchasIt pauses and asks you. Captcha-solving services are how accounts get flagged.
Judge legalityIt enforces scheduling hygiene, not duty-time regulations. The portal is authoritative.
Change your baseWrong domicile means being rostered out of a city you don't live in. Hard-blocked.
GuessAn unreadable position, unknown base, or unreachable page results in an alert — never a claim.

Getting it

Desktop app. Download, unzip, run. No Python, no install, no admin rights.

From source.

git clone https://github.com/djbatalona06/airline-shift-agent-.git
cd airline-shift-agent-
python -m venv .venv
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pip install -e .
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m playwright install chromium
python -m shift_agent.main demo

Full instructions, including running it 24/7 on a small server: the install guide.

Before you use this. Automated shift pickup may be restricted by your employer's terms, your union agreement, or the portal's terms of service. Check first — you are responsible for how you use it.

Privacy

It never receives your password: you sign in yourself and it works with the session you created. Anything it does keep goes in your operating system's credential store. Nothing is sent anywhere except the portal you configure and, if you set one up, your own Telegram bot. There is no telemetry.
